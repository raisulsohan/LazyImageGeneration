============================================================
  LAZY-IMAGE
  AI image generation inside After Effects and Premiere Pro
  Free and open source
============================================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Windows 10 or 11
  - Adobe After Effects and/or Premiere Pro (CC 2019 or newer)
  - Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
  - A ChatGPT account (free or paid)


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - INSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Unzip this download anywhere. The Desktop is fine.
  2. Close After Effects and Premiere Pro if they are open.
  3. Double-click  install.bat
  4. Wait for "Installed", then press a key.

  The panel is signed, so nothing else needs installing and
  no Adobe setting has to be changed.


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - LOG IN TO CHATGPT (ONLY ONCE)
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Open After Effects or Premiere Pro.
  2. Go to:  Window  >  Extensions  >  Lazy-Image
  3. Click "Login to ChatGPT" in the panel.
  4. A browser window opens. Log in as you normally would.
  5. The window closes by itself once you are logged in.

  Lazy-Image is free. There is no activation key to enter.

  You will not need to do this again. Lazy-Image uses its own
  separate browser profile, so your everyday browser, bookmarks
  and logins are not touched.


------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Save your project first, so images are stored next to it.
  2. Open the composition (After Effects) or the sequence
     (Premiere Pro) you want the image to go into.
  3. Type what you want, choose an aspect ratio, and click
     "Generate Image".  (Ctrl+Enter also works.)

  Each image is:
    - saved in a "chatgptimages" folder next to your project
    - added to a "ChatGptImages" folder/bin in your project
    - placed on your timeline at the playhead

  No browser window appears while generating. Chrome or Edge
  runs hidden only while the image is being made, then closes.
  It never takes keyboard focus away from Adobe.

  Prompts work in any language, including Bangla.


------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
  Click the "Test" button in the panel first. It checks the
  browser, your ChatGPT login and the ChatGPT page, and tells
  you exactly what is missing. It does not use up any images.

  The panel is not in the Window menu
      Restart After Effects / Premiere Pro. Adobe only looks
      for new panels while the app is starting.

  The panel opens blank
      Run "Fix a blank panel.bat" and restart the app. On some
      computers Adobe's signature check fails even for a
      correctly signed panel; this tells Adobe to load it anyway.

  "Login required"
      Click "Login to ChatGPT" and log in again.

  "ChatGPT wants a quick human verification"
      Click "Open ChatGPT", finish the check in the browser
      window, close the window, then generate again.

  "ChatGPT has hit a usage limit"
      Your ChatGPT account has run out of images for now. Wait
      for it to reset, or use a paid ChatGPT plan.

  "ChatGPT has changed its website"
      Email me. ChatGPT changed something and an update is needed.


------------------------------------------------------------
UNINSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------
  Double-click  uninstall.bat
  It asks before removing your saved ChatGPT login as well.
